A vendor deposit recorded in SKU.io now flows through to your accounting system, so prepaid supplier balances are reflected in your books without re-keying.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **QuickBooks Online** — a vendor deposit posts as a journal entry, and the QBO reference is stored back on the deposit so the two stay linked.
- **Xero** — a vendor deposit posts as a prepayment, with the Xero prepayment ID recorded on the deposit.
- The push runs in the background as a tracked job, so a slow accounting API never blocks your work.

Prepayments are no longer a spreadsheet living outside your ledger — record the deposit once in SKU.io and it lands in QuickBooks or Xero on its own.
