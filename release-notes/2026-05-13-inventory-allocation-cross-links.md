The **Inventory Allocation Pipeline** tables now cross-link every entity
they show — supplier names, product SKUs, sales orders, and the
"Covered by PO" chip all jump to the corresponding detail page. A new
chart-line drilldown icon next to supplier and product rows opens the
matching analysis tab without losing your place.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Investigating a coverage gap no longer requires copy-pasting an SKU
  into the URL bar — click straight through.
- The drilldown icon switches between Allocations / Product Analysis /
  Supplier Analysis tabs in place, keeping the same filters applied.

Find the new links across **Inventory → Allocation Pipeline** —
Allocations table, Product Analysis tab, and Supplier Analysis tab.
