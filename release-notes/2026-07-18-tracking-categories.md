If you sell on more than one channel, your Xero or QuickBooks profit and
loss report probably can't tell you which channel actually makes money —
Amazon revenue, Shopify revenue and your wholesale orders all land in the
same sales and cost-of-goods accounts. Until now the only way to split
them out was to create a separate account for every channel, which bloats
your chart of accounts and makes month-end a chore. **SKU.io now supports
Tracking Categories** — map each of your sales channels to a Xero tracking
category option (or a QuickBooks class), and every invoice, credit and
cost-of-goods journal we post carries that tag. Run your provider's normal
P&L-by-tracking-category report and your numbers are already split by
channel — no extra accounts required.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **Profit and loss by channel** — see revenue *and* margin for Amazon
  vs Shopify vs wholesale in your own Xero or QuickBooks reports, using
  the tags SKU.io attaches automatically.
- **COGS by channel, not just revenue** — the cost-of-goods journals
  SKU.io generates from your real fulfilment costs carry the channel tag
  too, so you get true gross margin per channel — not just top-line sales.
- **No account proliferation** — one tracking category with an option per
  channel replaces a whole column of duplicate sales and COGS accounts.
- **Set it once** — map each channel to an option in a single settings
  screen; from then on every synced document is tagged for you.
- **Works with what you already have** — pulls your existing Xero
  tracking categories (or QuickBooks classes) so you map to the ones your
  accountant already uses.

## Map each channel to a tracking category

Open the new **Tracking Categories** tab in your accounting settings.
Pick the Xero tracking category you want to segment by — most sellers use
one named "Sales Channel" — and match each of your connected channels to
one of its options. Anything left unmapped is flagged so nothing gets
tagged by accident, and you can set a fallback for channels you haven't
mapped yet.

![The Tracking Categories settings screen, mapping each sales channel to a Xero tracking category option](/img/release-notes/tracking-categories/tracking-categories-panel.png)

Choose exactly which documents get tagged — revenue, cost of goods, and
Amazon settlement postings — with a simple set of toggles, so the tagging
matches how you want to read your reports.

## Create options without leaving SKU.io

Missing an option for a channel? You don't have to jump over to Xero and
back. Create the tracking option — or a whole new tracking category —
right from the settings screen, and SKU.io creates it in Xero for you and
maps it in one step. (SKU.io respects Xero's limit of two active tracking
categories, so it'll tell you when you've reached it.)

## QuickBooks Online too

On a QuickBooks connection the same screen maps your channels to
**classes** instead of tracking categories, so QuickBooks sellers get the
same profit-and-loss-by-channel view using class tracking.

## Where to find it

Go to **Accounting → Settings → Tracking Categories** (it appears once
you've connected Xero or QuickBooks Online). Map your channels, save, and
your next synced invoices and cost-of-goods journals will be tagged.
