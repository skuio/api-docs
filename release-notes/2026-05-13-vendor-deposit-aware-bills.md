Purchase invoices are now **vendor-deposit aware**. When you create or
open a bill from a supplier with available deposits, a new **Bill
Deposits** panel lists the eligible deposits and lets you apply them
line-by-line, in bulk, or have them auto-applied as invoice lines save.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- The bookkeeper no longer has to reconcile prepayments manually — the
  bill shows the supplier's available deposit balance right next to the
  line items.
- Auto-apply uses the supplier's payment-terms milestone schedule (where
  configured) to pick the right deposit for each line.
- The invoice footer summary surfaces deposit-applied totals, so the
  remaining balance due is always accurate.

Find the new panel on any purchase invoice — **Orders → Purchase
Invoices → Create / open invoice**.
