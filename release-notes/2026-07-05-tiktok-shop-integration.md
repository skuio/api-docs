If you sell on TikTok Shop, you already know the tax it charges on your
time: every order re-keyed into SKU by hand, stock levels that drift out
of sync until a listing oversells, tracking numbers copied back into
Seller Center one by one, and returns that stay invisible until someone
reconciles them weeks later. TikTok's on-time-shipping metrics don't
forgive any of it. **TikTok Shop is now a first-class sales channel in
SKU** — connect your seller account once and orders import
automatically, available inventory pushes out on a schedule, tracking
submits back the moment you fulfill, and returns and refunds land where
you can see them. It lives right alongside Shopify, Amazon, and the rest,
using the exact same screens you already know.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **Orders import automatically** — every TikTok Shop order becomes a
  SKU sales order on a schedule and in real time, so inventory deducts
  the moment a sale happens instead of when you get around to typing it in.
- **Inventory pushes back to TikTok** — available stock syncs out per
  warehouse, so your listings stop overselling and you stop eating
  cancellations and seller-score penalties.
- **Tracking writes itself back** — fulfill an order in SKU and the
  tracking number is submitted to TikTok for you, keeping your on-time
  shipping metrics healthy without the copy-paste.
- **Self-serve product mapping** — link TikTok listings to your SKU
  products with per-row mapping, a bulk modal, CSV import/export, Smart
  Match suggestions, and one-click product creation for anything new.
- **Returns and refunds stay reconciled** — customer returns and refunds
  sync in automatically, so your inventory and accounting reflect them
  without a manual pass.
- **One UI for every channel** — TikTok Shop uses the same navigation,
  tables, filters, and detail pages as your other integrations, so
  there's nothing new to learn.

## A dashboard for your TikTok Shop at a glance

Connect your TikTok Shop seller account and you get a dedicated
workspace with its own dashboard — order counts, product counts, and
recent-sales totals up top, with quick links into every part of the
channel. Sync runs on a schedule you control, and a countdown tells you
exactly when the next automatic sync will fire.

## Orders flow in automatically

Every TikTok Shop order imports as a SKU sales order, complete with
buyer details, line items, and its current TikTok status — awaiting
shipment, in transit, delivered, completed. The orders list is the same
sortable, filterable table with the same checkbox column and bulk
actions you use on every other channel, so you can triage and fulfill
TikTok orders exactly the way you already work.

![The TikTok Shop orders list with buyer, status, totals, and bulk actions](/img/release-notes/tiktok-shop-integration/orders-list.png)

## Map your listings your way

Before inventory can sync and orders can deduct the right stock, TikTok
listings need to be linked to your SKU products — and mapping is fully
self-serve. Each listing shows whether it's mapped, and unmapped ones
are one click away from being linked. When you have a lot to do, the bulk
mapping modal and a CSV download-and-upload roundtrip let you handle them
all at once.

![The TikTok Shop products list showing mapped SKUs alongside unmapped listings ready to map](/img/release-notes/tiktok-shop-integration/products-mapping.png)

The mapping modal does the heavy lifting for you. **Smart Match** suggests
the most likely SKU product for each listing, so most mappings are a
single confirmation. If a listing has no equivalent in SKU yet, create a
new product from it right there. And for non-inventory line items — fees,
gift wrapping, warranties — you can map to a **revenue line** instead of a
product, so they post to accounting correctly instead of distorting your
stock.

![The TikTok Shop mapping modal with map-to-product and map-to-revenue-line options, Smart Match suggestions, and create-new-product](/img/release-notes/tiktok-shop-integration/mapping-modal.png)

## Where to find it

Open **Integrations** and add **TikTok Shop**, then authorize your seller
account. Once connected, your TikTok Shop lives under **Integrations →
TikTok Shop**, with tabs for Dashboard, Orders, Products, Returns,
Refunds, Transactions, Webhooks, and Settings — the same layout as every
other sales channel in SKU.
