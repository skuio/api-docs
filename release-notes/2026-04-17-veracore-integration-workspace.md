The Veracore fulfillment integration now has a complete, dedicated management workspace.

<!-- truncate -->

Connecting Veracore previously gave you only a single page to work from. The integration now opens into a full workspace — a dashboard with order and sync health at a glance, plus dedicated screens for orders, order detail, report tasks, shipping methods, and source-code mappings. Order syncing and fulfillment cost reconciliation run as tracked background jobs you can follow in the job tray. Open it from Integrations and select your Veracore connection.
