## How to use this page

- **Most recent updates are at the top.** Scroll down, or open the [archive](/release-notes/archive) to browse by date.
- **Filter by what matters to you.** Every update is tagged by change type (New, Improved, Fixed) and by product area (Orders, Inventory, Purchasing, Integrations, and more). Click any tag on a post to see only the areas you work in.
- **Search.** Use the search bar to find when a specific feature shipped.
- **Subscribe.** An RSS feed is available at `/release-notes/rss.xml` so you never miss an update.

Each entry leads with a short "what this means for you" summary, so you can scan quickly and dig into the detail only where you need it.
