SKU.io can now publish your purchasing commitments straight into Google Calendar, so upcoming outgoings show up next to the rest of your schedule.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Connect a Google account and subscribe a calendar to one or both feeds:
  - **PO Cash Flow** — when payments on purchase orders are expected.
  - **Vendor Deposits** — when supplier prepayment installments come due.
- Events are kept in sync automatically as dates change in SKU.io — due dates move, the calendar moves with them.
- Anyone you share that Google Calendar with sees the same upcoming commitments, no SKU.io login required.

Connect a calendar under **Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar**.
