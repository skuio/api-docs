Vendor deposits can now require approval before they are paid. Define who signs off — and in what order — so prepayments to suppliers go through the same controls as the rest of your purchasing.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Configure **approval levels** and the **approval steps** within them, so a deposit can need one sign-off or several in sequence.
- A deposit awaiting sign-off sits in **Pending Approval** and cannot be paid until it clears — the approver, timestamp, and any approval notes are recorded on the deposit.
- Approve deposits one at a time, or select several and **approve them in bulk**.

This keeps larger prepayments visible to the right people before any money leaves, without slowing down the small routine ones. Approval levels are managed in **Purchasing → Vendor Deposits → Settings**.
