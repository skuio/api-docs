Until now, listings only ever flowed *into* SKU.io. To actually put a product up for sale you still had to log into each channel's seller portal — eBay Seller Hub, Shopify admin, Amazon Seller Central, Walmart — and re-key the same title, description, images, price, and attributes, once per channel. And because every marketplace requires different fields per category, you often didn't discover a missing attribute until the channel rejected the listing.

**Multichannel Listing & PIM** closes that gap. SKU.io now doubles as a Product Information Management hub that can **publish new listings to** and **update existing listings on** every connected sales channel — so your catalog is the single source of truth and each channel stays in sync with it.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **Publish from one place.** Send a product to eBay, Shopify, Amazon, or Walmart without leaving SKU.io — no more re-keying the same data into four seller portals.
- **See your whole catalog's coverage at a glance.** One matrix shows every product's status on every channel — live, publishing, no longer live, or not yet configured.
- **Catch problems before the channel does.** Channel-required, category-specific fields are surfaced *before* you publish, so listings stop getting rejected for missing attributes.
- **Keep channels in sync.** When a product's details change in SKU.io, push the update out to the live listing instead of hand-editing each channel.
- **Manage the whole lifecycle.** End a listing on a channel, retry a failed publish, or bulk-publish a set of products — all from inside SKU.io.

## One home for every channel listing

The new **Listings** workspace brings every channel listing into a single, filterable table. Switch between all channels or drill into just Amazon, eBay, Shopify, or Walmart, and see each listing's SKU, title, channel, price, and stock-of-record side by side.

![The Listings workspace showing product listings across Amazon, eBay, Shopify, and Walmart in one table](/img/release-notes/multichannel-listings/live-listings.png)

## See your whole catalog's coverage at a glance

The **Catalog Coverage** view answers the question every multichannel seller asks: *what's live where, and what still needs attention?* Each product gets a row, each connected channel gets a column, and every cell shows that product's real publish state on that channel — **live**, **publishing**, **removed**, or not yet configured. The summary cards up top roll it all up so you can jump straight to what needs work.

![The Catalog Coverage matrix showing each product's publish status across four channels, with summary cards for live, publishing, and not-configured listings](/img/release-notes/multichannel-listings/catalog-coverage.png)

## Templates make repeat publishing effortless

The first time you publish to a channel you map your product data onto that channel's fields. Save those choices as a **template** — a reusable bundle of category, field defaults, and pricing profile for a channel — and every subsequent publish becomes pick-a-template-and-review. The 2nd, 3rd, and Nth product go live in seconds instead of minutes.

## Where to find it

Open **Inventory → Listings** in the left sidebar. Use the **Live Listings** and **Catalog Coverage** tabs at the top to switch between your live listings and the coverage matrix, and the **Templates** tab to manage your reusable publish templates.
