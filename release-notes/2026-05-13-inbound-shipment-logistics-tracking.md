Inbound shipments — especially for sea-freight and international air — gain
a structured logistics layer. Define your own milestone types in Settings
(ETD, port ETA, customs cleared, warehouse ETA, container loaded, anything
your operation tracks), then capture both estimated and actual dates per
shipment.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- A new **Logistics Timeline** renders the milestone progression on every
  inbound shipment and on the parent PO's shipments tab, with late
  shipments flagged automatically.
- Multi-carrier tracking-number entry sits alongside the timeline so you
  can record DHL + ocean carrier + drayage references on the same shipment.
- The recorded ETAs feed the new **Purchasing Cash Flow Schedule** and
  shift payment-term milestone due dates as actuals come in.

You'll find the new fields under **Settings → Purchasing → Logistics
Tracking Fields**, and the new timeline on each **Inbound Shipment** detail
page. Existing date columns on PO shipments have been migrated into
tracking-field values automatically.
