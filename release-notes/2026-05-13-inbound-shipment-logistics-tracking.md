Inbound shipments can now be tracked against **logistics milestones you define yourself** — the steps your freight actually goes through, in the order they happen.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Create named logistics tracking fields — for example *Booked*, *Departed Port*, *Customs Cleared*, *Arrived* — each with a description and a position in the pipeline.
- Record the date each milestone is reached on an inbound shipment, giving everyone a shared, ordered view of where goods are.
- Retire a milestone you no longer use by deactivating it, without losing the history on past shipments.
- Logistics milestones can also **trigger vendor deposit installments** — so a payment can fall due the moment a shipment clears customs, not on a guessed calendar date.

Tracking fields are managed in **Purchasing → Settings → Logistics Tracking**.
