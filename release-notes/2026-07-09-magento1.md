Still running a Magento 1 (or OpenMage) storefront? It hasn't had an
official update in years, but plenty of stores still sell on it every
day — and keeping it in step with the rest of your operation has meant
re-keying every order by hand and hoping your published stock levels
haven't drifted since the last time someone checked. **Magento 1 is now
a first-class sales channel in SKU** — connect your store once and orders
import automatically, your catalog syncs in, and a side-by-side inventory
view shows exactly where your real stock and your storefront disagree so
you can push the correct numbers back with one click. It lives right
alongside Shopify, Amazon, and every other channel, using the same
screens you already know.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **Orders import automatically** — every Magento 1 order becomes a SKU
  sales order on a schedule, so stock deducts when a sale actually happens
  instead of when you get around to typing it in.
- **Your catalog syncs in** — products pull from Magento 1 into SKU, ready
  to map and manage next to everything else you sell.
- **A true stock comparison, side by side** — one screen shows your SKU
  quantity next to your live Magento 1 quantity for every item, and tells
  you exactly which ones are out of sync and by how much.
- **Push the right numbers back** — sync corrected inventory out to
  Magento 1 in one action, so your storefront stops overselling stock you
  don't have.
- **No plugin, no webhooks to babysit** — Magento 1 has no real-time
  events, so SKU polls your store for you on a schedule you control. There's
  nothing to install on the Magento side.
- **One UI for every channel** — Magento 1 uses the same navigation,
  tables, and filters as your other integrations, so there's nothing new
  to learn.

## A dashboard for your store at a glance

Connect your Magento 1 store and you get a dedicated workspace with its
own dashboard — order and product totals up top, how many items are in
sync versus pending a push, and the status of the last poll for orders
and products. Because Magento 1 is poll-based rather than event-driven, a
banner reminds you that the figures reflect your last completed sync, so
you always know how fresh the numbers are.

![The Magento 1 integration dashboard showing total orders and products, inventory-in-sync counts, last-sync status, and quick actions](/img/release-notes/magento1/dashboard.png)

## Orders flow in automatically

Every Magento 1 order imports as a SKU sales order, complete with the
customer, line items, totals, and its current Magento status — pending,
processing, complete, holded, or canceled. The orders list is the same
sortable, filterable table you use on every other channel, with rich
filters for status, product mapping, currency, and shipping method. Turn a
Magento order into a SKU sales order individually or in bulk, and pull the
latest straight from the storefront whenever you want.

![The Magento 1 orders list with customer, status, totals, and create-SKU-order actions](/img/release-notes/magento1/orders.png)

## See exactly where your stock disagrees — then fix it

This is the part that saves the most time. Instead of guessing whether
your storefront is accurate, the inventory view puts your **SKU quantity**
right next to your live **Magento 1 quantity** for every item, flags each
one as In Sync, Needs Update, or Needs Recalc, and shows the exact change
required to bring them back in line. When you're ready, push the corrected
numbers back to Magento 1 in a single action — no spreadsheet, no
item-by-item edits in the Magento admin.

![The Magento 1 inventory view comparing SKU quantities against live Magento 1 quantities, with change-needed badges and a sync-to-store action](/img/release-notes/magento1/inventory.png)

## Sync on your schedule

Everything runs on a schedule you control. From the channel's settings you
can turn automatic sync on or off, and see the status and timing of the
last orders and products poll at a glance. Prefer to run it by hand? Every
sync — orders, products, and inventory — is also available as a one-click
action.

![The Magento 1 sync settings with the automatic-sync toggle and last-poll status for orders and products](/img/release-notes/magento1/settings.png)

## Where to find it

Open **Apps & Integrations** and add **Magento 1**, then enter your store
URL and API credentials. Once connected, your store lives under
**Integrations → Magento 1**, with tabs for Dashboard, Orders, Products,
Inventory, Activity Log, and Settings — the same layout as every other
sales channel in SKU.
