A new view for finance teams: the **Purchasing Cash Flow Schedule** rolls
up every open purchase order and pending vendor-deposit milestone into a
single forward-looking cash outflow projection.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- The **Cash Due Schedule** lists every obligation by due date, grouped
  by week, so AP knows exactly what's coming up.
- The **12-Week Cash Flow Forecast** charts total outflows alongside
  available cash signals.
- An **Undated Obligations** card surfaces POs whose payment-term
  milestones don't yet have a triggering date — chase those down before
  they slip into "overdue".
- A standalone **Upcoming Liability** view focuses on the next 30 days
  for daily standups.

Find it at **Orders → Purchase Orders → Cash Flow Schedule**, with sibling
views under **Orders → Vendor Deposits → Cash Due / Cash Flow Forecast /
Upcoming Liability**.
