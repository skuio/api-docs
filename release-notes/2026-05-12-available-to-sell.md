Inventory now distinguishes **Available to Sell** from On Hand and Total Inventory, so the number you sell against is the number that is genuinely free to promise.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **Available to Sell** is shown alongside On Hand on the product page, the product inventory page, and in Demand Planning — derived directly from your stock columns and floored at zero, so it never reads negative.
- The terminology around on-hand versus total inventory has been clarified throughout, so the same word means the same thing on every screen.
- Allocations and inventory-planning views read from the same figures, keeping reports consistent with what you see on a product.

The result: fewer oversells from quoting a number that was actually committed elsewhere.
