Every business has that one question the standard reports can't quite answer — "on-hand value by product this month", "revenue by channel and brand", "which SKUs are moving and what are they worth". Until now, answering it meant exporting to a spreadsheet or asking someone to build a report for you. The new **Custom Report Builder** lets you answer those questions yourself: pick the data, drag the fields you care about, and get a live table, chart, or pivot in seconds — no spreadsheets, no waiting.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Build reports across **Sales, Inventory, Purchasing, Financial, Returns, Logistics, and Integrations** — all in one place.
- **Drag and drop** dimensions and measures into rows, columns, and values — or just **ask in plain English** and let SKU build it for you.
- Flip the same result between **table, chart, and pivot** without re-running anything.
- Add your own **calculated columns** with a guided formula editor and live validation.
- **Save, share, and schedule** reports so the numbers you care about arrive automatically.

## Start with any corner of your business

Pick a **domain** — Sales, Inventory, Purchasing, Financial, and more — then a starting table, and SKU opens a searchable catalog of every field you can report on, complete with plain-English descriptions so you always know what a field means. Blue fields are things you group by; orange fields are numbers that add up.

![The report builder showing the data-source picker, searchable field catalog, and the rows/columns/values/filters shelves](/img/release-notes/custom-report-builder/builder.png)

## Build it by dragging — or just ask

Drag fields onto the **Rows**, **Columns**, **Values**, and **Filters** shelves to shape your report. Measures aggregate automatically (sum, average, count, and more), and grand totals and subtotals are on by default. Prefer to skip the setup? Type what you want into the **Ask AI** bar — "on-hand value by product", "revenue by category and channel" — and SKU assembles the report for you to fine-tune.

## See it as a table, chart, or pivot — instantly

Run the report and the results come back with totals, heat-map shading, and data bars built in. Switch between **table**, **pivot**, and **chart** views with one click — the same data, visualized the way you need it — and reshape on the fly with options like *show values as % of total*, *rank*, and *top / bottom N*.

![A finished report showing on-hand quantity and stock value by product, with totals, conditional formatting, and a bar chart](/img/release-notes/custom-report-builder/results.png)

## Add your own calculated columns

Need a number that isn't in the data yet — average unit value, margin %, revenue per unit? Open the formula editor, give your column a name and format, and write an expression using a catalog of available measures and supported functions. SKU validates it as you type, so you know it's right before you add it.

![The formula editor with a valid calculated column and the catalog of available measures](/img/release-notes/custom-report-builder/formula-editor.png)

## Save, share, and schedule

Save a report to your library and it's a click away next time. Share it with your team, or set up a **schedule** so it's delivered automatically on a recurring basis — no more remembering to pull the numbers each week.

![The Custom Reports library listing a saved report with its domain, owner, and last-run details](/img/release-notes/custom-report-builder/reports-hub.png)

## Where to find it

Head to **Insights → Reports → Custom** in the main navigation, then click **New Report** to start from scratch — or open one you've already saved.
