Two long-running quality-of-life additions for Demand Planning:

1. You can now **star a saved configuration as your personal default** —
   it auto-loads the next time you open Demand Planning, so you don't have
   to pick the same config every visit.
2. Rerunning an existing forecast now supports a **Preview mode** that
   regenerates the recommendations and charts without dispatching POs,
   sending alerts, or advancing the schedule.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Teams with several saved configurations (one per channel, brand, or
  warehouse, for example) can pick their primary view once and stop
  hunting through the dropdown each session. Other users on the same
  tenant keep their own defaults.
- Before committing to a fresh run that would create dozens of POs or
  fire backorder alerts, you can preview the new recommendations against
  the latest sales and inventory data and double-check before publishing.
- The custom date-range toggle on the Sales History chart and data table
  also tightens up: turning it off immediately reverts to trailing days
  instead of leaving a stale custom range visible.

Open **Demand Planning**, expand the saved-configurations menu, and pick
the new star icon to mark your default; on a forecast detail page the
**Rerun** action now lets you choose Preview or Full Run.
