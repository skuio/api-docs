You tweak a kit's recipe — swap a component, bump a quantity — and suddenly your history and your forecasts look different, as if the previous version never existed. Worse, you can't answer a simple question: *what was actually in this bundle back in the spring?* Until now, a bundle or kit had exactly one recipe — whatever it happens to be right now. **Recipe History** changes that. Every bundle and kit now keeps a full, dated history of its recipe, so you can see what it contained on any date, edit it without rewriting the past, schedule a change ahead of time, and correct a past recipe when something was recorded wrong.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **A full timeline of every recipe change** — every version of a bundle or kit's recipe, with what changed, when, and who made it.
- **See the recipe as of any date** — pick a date and SKU.io shows exactly what the product contained then.
- **Editing no longer rewrites the past** — a change applies from today forward, so last quarter's records stay exactly as they were.
- **Schedule a change ahead of time** — set a new recipe to take effect on a future date; today's recipe stays live until then, then switches over automatically.
- **Correct the past when you need to** — fix a recipe that was recorded wrong for a stretch of time, clearly flagged as a correction, and bring the affected orders back in line.

## A full history of every recipe

Every bundle and kit now has a **Composition** tab that shows its recipe as a timeline. Each entry is a dated version — active now, superseded, scheduled for later, or a correction — with a plain-language note of what changed and who made it. Alongside it, the **Effective Recipe** panel shows exactly what's in the product today. No more guessing whether the recipe you're looking at is the one that was in effect last season.

![The Composition tab for a kit, showing a Revision History timeline of dated recipe versions on the left and the current Effective Recipe on the right](/img/release-notes/recipe-history/composition-timeline.png)

## See the recipe as of any date

Set the **As of** date and the Effective Recipe updates to show what the product contained on that day. A kit that ships with three components today might have had two last summer — and now that's a single click to confirm, not an archaeology project. Because the history is real, a recipe you edit today never changes what the product looked like on an earlier date.

![The Effective Recipe panel resolved as of a past date, showing the two components the kit contained then — before a later component was added](/img/release-notes/recipe-history/as-of-viewer.png)

## Schedule a change ahead of time

Planning a formulation update or a seasonal edition? Open **New recipe change**, adjust the components, and set the **effective date** in the future. SKU.io schedules it: the live recipe stays exactly as it is until that day arrives, then the new one takes over on its own. You can line up a change weeks ahead without it affecting anything you sell today.

![The New recipe change dialog with a future effective date, showing a "Scheduled — the live recipe stays unchanged until then" confirmation](/img/release-notes/recipe-history/schedule-change.png)

## Correct the past when you need to

Sometimes a recipe was simply recorded wrong for a stretch of time. Set the effective date in the **past** and confirm, and SKU.io files it as a correction — changing only that window's history, and marking it clearly so the change is always attributable. For bundles, you can take it a step further: SKU.io lists every order placed in that window — with each one's status and the exact **before → after** for each component — so you can bring the affected orders in line with the corrected recipe.

![The affected-orders step after a back-dated bundle correction, listing the orders in that window with their status and a before-to-after change for each component](/img/release-notes/recipe-history/rewrite-orders.png)

## Where to find it

Open any **bundle** or **kit** product and select the **Composition** tab. Your product's current recipe is on the right; its full history is on the left; and **New recipe change** lets you edit it now, schedule it for later, or correct it in the past.
