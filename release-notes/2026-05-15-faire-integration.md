Faire joins SKU as a first-class sales channel — connect your account and orders, products, and inventory stay in sync.

<!-- truncate -->

Add a Faire connection from the Integrations menu and SKU pulls your Faire orders, listings, and inventory into a dedicated workspace — with an orders list and detail view, product mapping to match Faire listings to SKU products, and inventory sync. Synced Faire orders now turn into SKU sales orders automatically as they arrive, so nothing waits on a manual import step.
