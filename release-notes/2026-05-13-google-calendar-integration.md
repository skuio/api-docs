A brand-new integration: connect your Google Calendar to SKU.io and
subscribe to **PO cash-flow milestones** and **vendor-deposit due dates**.
Events flow into your calendar automatically, with two-way deletion so
your calendar stays clean as deposits are paid or shipments arrive.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Finance and operations leads can keep their personal calendar as the
  single source of truth for "what's due this week" without manually
  copying dates from SKU.io.
- Subscribe per-feature: opt in just to PO obligations, or just to
  vendor-deposit milestones, or both.
- When a deposit is paid or a milestone is cancelled in SKU.io, the
  corresponding calendar event is removed automatically.

Connect at **Integrations → Google Calendar**.
