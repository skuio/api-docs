You can now record money paid to a supplier **before** a bill exists — a deposit, prepayment, or down payment — and track it through its whole life until it is fully applied or refunded. Vendor deposits sit alongside Purchase Orders and bills, so prepaid balances are never lost track of again.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Capture a deposit the moment you pay a supplier, optionally tied to a specific Purchase Order, with its own deposit number, date, due date, and notes.
- Work in the supplier's currency — each deposit stores its currency, exchange rate, and a tenant-currency snapshot, so reporting stays accurate even when rates move.
- Record tax on the deposit (per-line tax breakdown and a total), record one or more payments against it, and **apply** the deposit to bills as they arrive.
- Refund an unused balance or write off a remainder — nothing has to be left dangling.

Each deposit moves through a clear status lifecycle — **Draft → Pending Approval → Approved → Partially Paid → Paid → Partially Applied → Fully Applied**, plus **Refunded**, **Written Off**, **Void**, and **Cancelled** — so at a glance you know exactly where a prepayment stands.

Deposits can be created individually, in bulk, or generated automatically for a Purchase Order. You'll find them under **Purchasing → Vendor Deposits**, and on the related Purchase Order and Supplier records.
