Supplier agreements often stage payment — some on order, some on shipment, the balance on delivery. Payment terms can now carry a **vendor-deposit schedule** that does exactly that, turning a deposit into installments that come due automatically as a Purchase Order moves forward.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- Add a deposit schedule to a payment term, splitting the prepayment into installments — for example 30% on order, 40% on shipment, 30% on receipt.
- Each installment is triggered by a real event: **PO approved**, **PO shipped**, **PO fully shipped**, **PO received**, **PO fully received**, a **logistics tracking milestone**, or simply **manual**.
- As a Purchase Order hits each stage, the matching installment's due date is set automatically — no manual diary-keeping.
- An **Expected Milestones** view shows, per Purchase Order, which deposit installments are due, paid, or still ahead — so you can see upcoming cash commitments at a glance.

Due dates stay in sync if the PO timeline shifts, so your prepayment plan always reflects reality.
