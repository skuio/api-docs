The new **Vendor Deposits** module ships with a full reports suite — eight
datatables covering the whole deposit lifecycle.

<!-- truncate -->

**✨ What this means for you:**

- **Aging Report** — buckets outstanding deposits by age so you can chase
  long-stale balances.
- **Supplier Exposure** — total outstanding deposit value per supplier.
- **Supplier Statement** — printable account-style statement per supplier.
- **Expected Milestones** — what's coming due, sortable by trigger date.
- **PO-by-Shipping-Stage** — deposits broken out by where their POs are
  in the logistics pipeline.
- **Exceptions Queue** — deposits flagged for missing data, FX mismatch,
  or stuck approval.
- **Write-Off Report** — full history of every write-off with reason and
  approver.
- **Upcoming Liability** — short-window cash forecast specific to deposits.

Each report is a filterable, exportable datatable. Find them as tabs
under **Orders → Vendor Deposits**.
