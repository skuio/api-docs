## How to use this page

- **Most recent changes are at the top.** Browse the [archive](/changelog/archive) for older entries.
- **Filter by change type.** Entries are tagged Added, Changed, Removed, Deprecated, and Breaking change — click any tag to filter.
- **Watch for breaking changes.** Anything that may break an existing integration is tagged `Breaking change` and called out at the top of the entry.
- **Subscribe.** An RSS feed is available at `/changelog/rss.xml`.

Entries here are generated mechanically from the published [OpenAPI specification](/docs/api/introduction) — they reflect the actual API surface, not intentions.
